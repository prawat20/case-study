# Revolut Primacy — Ideation & Selection

> Idea-generation and selection layer downstream of `revolut_painkiller_vitamin.md`. Seventeen ideas generated broadly across three idea zones (Trust counter-narrative · Primacy-as-ritual · Primacy-only economics), scored against a four-axis rubric (Impact · PLG-actionability · Differentiation · Interview-defensibility), narrowed to the two push-pull picks that ship in the caselet.

---

## TL;DR — the 2 picks

After ideating 17 ideas across the 3 idea zones from Stage 2 and scoring each on **Impact × PLG-actionability × Differentiation × Interview-defensibility**, the two highest-scoring + maximally-complementary picks are:

1. **"Confidence Period" — Open-Banking-powered parallel-run for the first 3 salary cycles.** *Push idea: de-risks the migration moment.* Salary lands in Revolut as primary, but the user opts-in to auto-mirror 30-50% to their existing high-street bank for the trust period. Bundled: a hard "Salary Guarantee" — if Revolut holds the deposit for AML review, the user receives an interest-free advance up to their salary amount within 60 minutes. After 3 successful cycles, auto-mirror fades; the user has *lived* primacy without the fear.
2. **"Locked Insights" — salary-aware intelligence surface, visible-but-locked for non-primary users.** *Pull idea: makes primacy unlock features that cannot work on a side-account.* Card-stack home surface: real intelligence ("Day 25 short by £400 — auto-pull from vault?" / "You're paying £37/mo on 3 unused subs" / "Move this DD payday +2, save £180"). Non-primacy users see the *card shapes* with blurred numbers + a single CTA: *"Connect your full income to unlock."* Data envy — they see what they're missing in their own currency.

These ideas are deliberately a **push-pull pair**. Confidence Period removes the friction of converting. Locked Insights creates the pull-reason for wanting to convert. Together they hit the C3 (considered-but-didn't) + C4 (switched-but-reverted) cohorts — the strategic target population identified in Stage 1.

Both ideas attack the pain categories with **no current vitamin counter** (P1 perceived operational risk) and the **components-exist-but-unmerchandised** vitamin (V2 salary-aware intelligence). They sit squarely inside PLG control — no licensing dependency, no capital moonshot.

## The broad ideation pass (17 ideas)

> Generated freely across the 3 idea zones. Tight one-liner each. Detail and scoring follow.

### Zone 1 — Trust counter-narrative (P1: perceived operational risk)

1. **Salary Guarantee** — If Revolut holds a salary deposit for AML review > X hrs, an interest-free advance up to the salary amount auto-deploys.
2. **Human Escalation Promise** — A primacy-only phone line with a guaranteed <5-minute human pickup during business hours. Solves the AI-bot-loop asymmetry directly.
3. **Salary Safe status indicator** — Persistent home-screen surface: ✓ "Your last 6 salaries cleared in 0 minutes. Last review: never." Turns invisible AML into visible reassurance.
4. **Pre-cleared Salary** — Open-Banking pull from the user's existing primary bank during the switch flow proves payroll history. Result: their salary mandate is pre-flagged "verified employer" and bypasses standard AML triggers for the next 12 cycles.
5. **Salary Insurance (paid)** — £1.99/mo. If your salary is held, instant advance + waived fees + human caseworker assigned. Insurance-product-shaped.
6. **Confidence Period (Salary Buddy)** — Open-Banking parallel-run: salary lands at Revolut, opt-in mirrors 30-50% to old bank for 3 cycles. Trust accrues by *living* primacy with a built-in fallback. Fades after 3 cycles.
7. **Public Freeze Statistics dashboard** — Monthly transparency: % of salary deposits held, median hold duration, % escalated, root-cause breakdown. Radical-transparency play.
8. **Frozen-Account Emergency Bypass** — If account is restricted, £500 (or 25% of typical month) remains accessible for essentials via a pre-locked emergency buffer.

### Zone 2 — Primacy-as-ritual (P4 + P5: switching friction + unmerchandised features)

9. **Salary Move (concierge migration)** — End-to-end flow: Open-Banking detect old primary, auto-import DDs, generate HR letter, guided checklist. The full migration product.
10. **"Revolut Primary" named tier/status** — Distinct from Metal/Ultra. Activated when salary + ≥3 DDs are routed. Unlocks a curated primacy dashboard. Make primacy itself a *feature with a name*.
11. **Bills Pots / Salary Sorter** — Monzo playbook copy: auto-distribute salary into spending + bills + savings the moment it lands. Revolut has Pockets; doesn't merchandise.
12. **Locked Insights** — Salary-aware intelligence surface (subs audit, payday-cashflow nudges, DD-payday optimization, projected-shortfall auto-vault-pull). Visible-but-locked for non-primary users with "connect full income to unlock" CTA. **Data envy as the conversion driver.**
13. **Reverse-CASS Onramp (partial switch)** — Acknowledge the cohabitation deal: keep old bank, set Revolut as parallel primary, flip month 3. Lower-stakes commitment. (Monzo did this in 2020.)
14. **Primacy Streak** — Gamified: months-in-a-row salary mandate; each month earns higher savings APR / free Metal time / bonus FX volume.
15. **Existing-User Primacy Bonus** — Retroactive reward for users who already moved their salary but got nothing (the t17 gap). One-time fix.

### Zone 3 — Primacy-only economics (V3 mistarget)

16. **Tier perks scale with primacy** — Re-architect: subscription fee is the floor; primacy is the multiplier (+X% savings APR, +Y% cashback, lounge access extends). Subscription stops being the only axis.
17. **Primacy Cashback** — Flat % cashback ONLY on debit-card spend funded by routed salary. Top-up money doesn't qualify. Aligns spend → debit → cashback through primacy.

## Why Confidence Period + Locked Insights (and not the other contenders)

### Why this pair, not the other tied scorer

The three 19-pointers are **Salary Guarantee** (#1), **Confidence Period** (#6), and **Locked Insights** (#12). Brief asks for 2, so something gets dropped. The selection rationale:

**Salary Guarantee gets absorbed into Confidence Period, not dropped.** During the Confidence Period (3 salary cycles with parallel-run to old bank), the user already has a built-in fallback at the old bank. The Salary Guarantee mechanism (interest-free advance on AML hold) is most valuable *during* this period, then continues *after* the period as a permanent primary-account perk. Treating it as a feature of Confidence Period rather than a standalone idea avoids the "5 features bundled into 1" trap from the brief while keeping the strongest individual mechanism in scope.

**Confidence Period + Locked Insights is the strongest push-pull pair** — they attack different mechanisms (migration de-risk vs only-on-primary value) and different cohorts:
- C3 (considered-but-didn't) → wants a low-stakes way to try → Confidence Period
- C4 (switched-but-reverted) → reverted because of the first-payday freeze → Confidence Period's parallel-run + Guarantee prevents this exact failure
- C2 (side-feature users) → won't move until there's a reason → Locked Insights creates the reason
- C1 (FX-driven, partial primary) → not fully on Revolut for daily life → Locked Insights deepens their primacy

A single idea (just Confidence Period) would miss the *pull* dimension; a single idea (just Locked Insights) would miss the C4 reversion-prevention dimension. The pair is non-redundant.

### Why the runner-ups don't make the cut

- **#4 Pre-cleared Salary** — close call. Stronger on *root-cause attack* (it actually reduces AML triggers, not just compensates for them). Dropped because PLG-feasibility scores lower: requires fraud/AML team coordination across organizational lines, longer-tail to ship. Confidence Period delivers the same trust effect using only Open Banking + product, no compliance-policy negotiation. Worth flagging as the natural Year-2 evolution of Confidence Period.
- **#8 Frozen-Account Bypass** — defensible and creative but narrower in scope: it only kicks in when something has *already* gone wrong. Confidence Period's Guarantee acts in the same window with a better mechanism (positive advance vs ring-fenced fragment).
- **#10 "Revolut Primary" tier/status** — high PLG-feasibility but lower impact on its own. Better as a *consequence* of the two picks: completing Confidence Period unlocks the "Primary" status badge + Locked Insights is one of its perks. The naming/branding sits inside the chosen pair, not as a standalone idea.
- **#16 Tier perks scale with primacy** — clean re-architecture but addresses V3 (under-leveraged vitamin) rather than P1 (highest-leverage pain). Lower-leverage zone.

### Why explicit non-picks

- **#9 Salary Move (concierge migration)** — solves the wrong problem. Research showed the dominant barrier is *fear of the first salary deposit*, not *the chore of switching*. CASS + HR letter already work mechanically; the user doesn't fail at *doing the chore*, they fail at *trusting the outcome*. This was my pre-research first instinct (rejected after Stage 1).
- **#11 Bills Pots / Salary Sorter** — pure Monzo copy with no differentiation. Necessary table-stakes, but doesn't earn ideation airtime in the brief's "2 best" slots. (Probably already on Revolut's 2026 roadmap regardless.)
- **#15 Existing-User Primacy Bonus** — direct violation of brief's *"can't be just paying for primacy."* Fixes a real gap (t17) but as a one-time payment, not a strategic feature.
- **#14 Primacy Streak / #17 Primacy Cashback** — same "paying for primacy" risk. Both add some leverage but not the *unique implementation* the brief asks for.

## How the two picks map to the rubric

| Rubric criterion (brief) | Confidence Period | Locked Insights |
|---|---|---|
| **Creativity** — type of ideas to deliver high impact on KPI | Acknowledges the two-bank cohabitation deal instead of denying it. Open-Banking parallel-run is novel vs incumbents. Salary Guarantee converts AML-hold from existential to manageable. | Locked-by-default surface that uses *data envy* as the conversion driver. Reframes primacy not as a sacrifice but as a key. Real intelligence, not a marketing animation. |
| **Depth** — clear understanding of how to implement + drive adoption | Open-Banking + parallel-run + advance mechanism + 3-cycle fade are all implementable today. Activation hooks: 2nd-largest-external-deposit detection, on the day a user clicks "make this primary." | Insights engine assembles existing Revolut components (Pockets + cashflow + spend categorization). Visible-locked surface uses real numbers if available else categorical blanks. Activation hooks: empty-state of Home, post-large-salary-detect banner. |
| **Analytical reasoning** — how it moves the needle | Attacks the demote-funnel (Pain P11) by giving users *evidence* that primacy works for them before they commit. Each "successful cycle" reduces reversion risk. Net effect: increases primacy-flip rate AND reduces post-flip reversion. | Attacks Pain P5 (primacy-hostile surface) + Vitamin V2 (unmerchandised salary-aware features). Net effect: creates an asymmetric reason to convert that Monzo / Starling / Chase cannot match — only Revolut has the cross-product correlation surface to produce these insights. |
| **Impact** — actual potential impact on KPIs | Strategic target: C3 + C4 cohorts. If Confidence Period converts 5% of C3 (≈500k UK users) over Year 1, that's a 5pp lift on UK primacy % from a single feature. | Strategic target: C2 + C3. Sustained pull-driver; pairs with Confidence Period to compound. Expect 2-3pp standalone, 3-5pp paired with #6. |
